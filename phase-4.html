Total Submersion Proof

We never ask clients to take our word for it. By simulating extreme torrential rainfall with 72-hour stagnant ponding, water tightness is physically proven prior to facility handover.

02

Digital Vector Precision

Electric field vector mapping directs low-voltage current across the wet membrane. Any pinhole creates an electrical circuit path to the structural ground, pinpointing the exact millimeter coordinate.
