Monolithic Membrane & TPO Application

Heated high-pressure plural component spray reactors atomize pure elastomeric polyurea at 3,000 PSI and 160°F. The monolithic coating cures in 15 seconds, creating a puncture-proof, seam-free elastomeric skin capable of bridging 3mm structural fissures.

01

15-Second Instant Cure

Unlike traditional polyurethane or bituminous roll sheets requiring days to cure, polyurea achieves full water resistance in 15 seconds. Sudden cloudbursts never wash away an uncured roof.

02

Zero Seams or Screws

Over 94% of commercial roof leaks happen at overlapping laps, mechanical fastener penetrations, or pipe flashings. Our monolithic lining envelops every pipe and drain seamlessly.

03

Dynamic Crack Bridging

With 450% ultimate elongation, the membrane stretches and breathes with thermal building expansion and contraction without tearing, cracking, or fatigue delamination.
