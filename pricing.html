Cost Breakdown

What Factors Influence Your Project Cost?

We believe in informed clients. Here are the 4 key variables that shape contractor pricing.

1. Square Footage & Pitch

Steeper roofs require specialized OSHA harness tie-offs, scaffolding, and longer labor hours. Flat terraces require calculated slope leveling to prevent ponding.

2. Substrate Condition

Hidden water damage to OSB or concrete screeds discovered during our drone scan will require replacement to ensure a solid foundation.

3. Membrane System Grade

Liquid polyurethane membranes provide 25+ years of elasticity compared to basic acrylic paints that break down under UV rays after 3 seasons.

4. Flashing & Edge Work

The number of chimneys, skylights, parapet corners, and HVAC curbs determines how many linear feet of custom solder or sealants are required.

Pricing & Warranty Questions

Is the 25-Year Warranty really transferable?

Yes! If you sell your home or building, the remaining warranty transfers to the new owner at zero cost upon filling out our simple 1-minute portal transfer form.

Does homeowner's insurance cover roof leak repairs?

If your roof damage resulted from storms, hail, high winds, or falling limbs, insurance typically covers the full replacement cost. We provide itemized drone thermal reports directly to your claims adjuster.

Are there any surprise fees or price escalations?

Never. Once our certified inspector presents your written proposal after the free thermal inspection, the price is 100% fixed and locked in.
