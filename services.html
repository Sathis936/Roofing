Common Service Questions

Why is liquid polyurethane superior to traditional torch-on felt?

Torch-on bituminous felt relies on overlapping seams every 1 meter, which deteriorate under UV radiation and seasonal thermal cycles. Liquid polyurethane forms an unbroken 100% seamless elastomeric membrane with 400% elongation, eliminating every possible seam failure point.

Can damaged gutters cause roof leaks or foundation problems?

Yes. Overflowing gutters saturate fascia boards and back up under roof drip edges, rotting structural rafters. Roof runoff pooling near exterior walls also causes foundation settling and basement seepage. Our seamless aluminum gutters channel stormwater safely away.

How does drone thermal scanning save building owners money?

Instead of recommending an unnecessary full-roof tear-off costing millions, thermal scans accurately isolate the exact saturated insulation pockets. This enables targeted surgical repairs and restorative coatings at up to 70% lower cost than full replacement.

What warranty coverage is included with your services?

Every full terrace restoration and complete re-roofing project is backed by our transferable 25-Year Leak-Proof Warranty covering both materials and labor. Workmanship warranties for emergency repairs are provided for 10 years.
