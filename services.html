Precision Roof Repair & Flashing Restoration

Roof leaks rarely travel straight down; water migrates along trusses, soaking thermal insulation and rotting structural plywood decking before appearing on your ceiling. Our repair protocols pinpoint and seal the root vulnerability.

Missing & Storm-Damaged Shingles: Class-4 impact-resistant architectural shingle replacements matched precisely to your existing roof pitch.
Chimney & Skylight Step Flashing: Replaced welded counter-flashing with heavy-gauge aluminum and high-durability elastomeric caulking.
Valley & Pipe Boot Sealing: High-stress water channels reinforced with ice-and-water self-adhering polymer membranes.

Terrace & Balcony Waterproofing Systems

Flat terraces and balconies are vulnerable to thermal expansion fractures, standing water pools, and porous screed degradation. We apply an unbroken 5-layer liquid polyurethane (PU) membrane that moves flexibly with the building.

Deep Penetrating Primer: Seals concrete pores and creates an unbreakable chemical bond with the substrate.
Fiberglass Geotextile Reinforcement: Bridges active movement cracks up to 3mm without rupturing or tearing.
Aliphatic UV Protective Topcoat: Reflects solar radiation, resists foot traffic, and withstands permanent standing water without peeling.

Seamless Aluminum Gutters & Leaf Defense

Clogged and sagging gutters are the #1 cause of rotten fascia boards, foundation shifts, and basement water intrusion. We custom-extrude seamless gutters directly at your property for an exact custom fit with zero leak-prone joints.

Continuous Seamless Extrusion: Fabricated on-site using rust-proof 0.032 heavy-gauge aluminum in 24 factory-baked color finishes.
Surgical Micro-Mesh Leaf Guards: Blocks pine needles, grit, and oak leaves while taking on up to 22 inches of rainfall per hour.
Oversized 3x4" High-Flow Downspouts: Discharges torrential stormwater away from perimeter landscaping and basement walls.

The 6-Step Watertight Delivery Process

How we ensure every single roofing and waterproofing project delivers zero leaks for 25 years.

01

Thermal Drone Scan

Non-invasive infrared scanning identifies hidden moisture traps and roof deck saturation before physical work begins.

02

Fixed-Price Estimate

Itemized scope of work with transparent materials list, timelines, and no surprise add-on charges.

03

Surface Preparation

High-pressure washing, mechanical grinding, rotten wood replacement, and epoxy fillet coving at all parapet junctions.

04

Multi-Layer Application

Installation of synthetic underlayments, seamless polyurethane liquid coats, and flashings per strict NRCA codes.

05

48-Hour Flood Test

We intentionally pond water on completed terraces for 48 continuous hours to prove 100% water tightness before sign-off.

06

25-Year Warranty Handover

Transferable legal warranty certificate issued to the property owner, fully registered in our digital portal.
