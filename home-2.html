Methodical Execution

The Vanguard Commercial Quality Protocol

Rigorous ISO-grade operational protocol guaranteeing zero disruption to your daily operations.

Phase 01

FLIR Drone & Core Sampling

We deploy thermal radiometric drones and non-destructive dielectric meters to map exact wet insulation boundaries across your entire facility.

Phase 02

Surface Profiling & Priming

Dust-free captive shot blasting and diamond grinding expose sound concrete (CSP 3-4 profile), followed by high-adhesion epoxy moisture-barrier primers.

Phase 03

Seamless Membrane Application

Heated high-pressure plural component spray reactors apply monolithic elastomeric polyurea or hot-air welded TPO with continuous thickness monitoring.

Phase 04

72-Hr Hydrostatic Certification

We perform continuous 72-hour flood tests and Electronic Vector Leak Detection (EVLD) to certify 100% pinhole-free integrity prior to warranty sign-off.
