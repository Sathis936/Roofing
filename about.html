Our Heritage

Why Traditional Roofing Fails—And How We Solve It

Over 80% of roof failures are not caused by defective shingles or tiles, but by flawed edge details, inadequate substrate preparation, and inferior caulking that cracks within 3 years of sun exposure.

At Vanguard, we treat roofs and terraces as interconnected building envelopes. By integrating high-molecular liquid polyurethane membranes, robotic thermal moisture scans, and custom continuous flashings, we eliminate every possible water entry route.

Our Operating Standard

The 4 Pillars of Vanguard Waterproofing

Every technician and project manager operates under strict non-negotiable protocols.

01

Substrate Precision

No membrane survives on dirty or compromised decks. We mechanically grind concrete, replace rotten wood decking, and epoxy-prime all surfaces.

02

Monolithic Application

Seams and joints are where 95% of roof leaks originate. Our liquid elastomeric PU systems cure into an unbroken, joint-free rubberized shield.

03

Scientific Verification

We deploy FLIR high-resolution thermal drones to detect moisture paths beneath shingles and conduct 48-hour continuous flood tests.

04

25-Year Guarantee

We don't provide vague verbal promises. Our work is backed by a registered legal certificate covering both materials and labor.
