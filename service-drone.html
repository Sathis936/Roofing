Pinpoint Wet Insulation Zones

Wet thermal insulation retains daytime solar heat longer than dry deck. High-sensitivity radiometric cameras capture this thermal inertia at twilight, outlining wet zones with millimeter accuracy.

🚫

Avoid Unnecessary Total Tear-Offs

Rather than paying for an expensive full roof tear-off, our engineering heat map enables surgical replacement of just the isolated saturated areas (often less than 8% of the roof), cutting capital expense by up to 70%.
