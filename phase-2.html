Phase 02 Substrate Preparation Standards

Certified per International Concrete Repair Institute (ICRI) Guideline No. 310.2R and ASTM D4541 hydraulic pull-off tests.

01

HEPA Filtered Dust Containment

Our closed-loop industrial shot blasting equipment operates with 99.97% HEPA vacuum recovery. Zero dust enters building HVAC air intakes or parking spaces.

02

Deep Chemical Keying

Epoxy primers penetrate up to 6mm into the concrete capillaries, fusing with the cementitious matrix to prevent future osmotic blistering or vapor debonding.

03

Verified Bond Guarantee

Every 2,000 square feet undergo calibrated hydraulic pull-off tests. The primer must fracture the concrete before the bond breaks, verifying unbreakable adhesion.
