Ideal for Mission-Critical Podium Plazas

100% Seamless Monolithic Membrane

Spray-applied with heated 2,500 psi hydraulic reactors directly over complex geometric shapes, planter box turn-ups, expansion joints, and pipe penetrations without a single vulnerable lap or seam.

Vehicular Deck Direct Wear Layer

Coupled with aluminum oxide or quartz aggregate broadcasting, polyurea forms an anti-skid vehicular traffic deck over multi-story parking garages resistant to brake fluid, de-icing salts, and tyre shear.

Green Roof Root-Proof Defense

Meets international FLL root resistance standards without toxic chemical biocides, making it the premier choice for luxury landscaped roof terraces, sod lawns, and deep soil planter architecture.
