01

Zero Destructive Punctures

Rather than blindly puncturing healthy roof membranes with dozens of core cuts, our thermal drones inspect 150,000 square feet non-destructively in under 45 minutes.

02

Eliminates False Assumptions

Moisture often travels 40 feet horizontally through insulation flutes before dripping inside. Drone thermography traces the water path directly back to the true breach source.

03

Saves Capital Budgets

Over 80% of commercial roofs condemned for complete replacement only have localized moisture saturation in 5% to 12% of the deck. We save millions in unnecessary tear-offs.
