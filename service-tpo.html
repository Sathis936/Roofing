System Benefits

Why Commercial Facilities Choose TPO

🔥

Continuous Robotic Hot-Air Seams

Seams are welded at 540°C with automated Leister crawlers, producing a monolithic molecular bond up to 4x stronger than cold-applied tapes or adhesives.

❄️

30% Cooling Load Reduction

The high-albedo solar-reflective surface repels 85% of incoming solar heat, dramatically decreasing chill plant operation costs across large roof areas.

⚡

Solar Array Compatible

Engineered specifically for non-penetrating solar ballast brackets or welded heat stanchions, allowing green energy integration without compromising watertight integrity.
