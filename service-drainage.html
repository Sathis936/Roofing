System Benefits

Zero Overflow Under Severe Deluge

Siphonic Vacuum Evacuation

Uses negative pressure to fill downpipes with 100% water (no air core), quadrupling discharge velocity and allowing smaller diameter horizontal runs without trenching.

Heavy-Duty Box Gutter Fabrication

Custom folded from continuous 1.6mm thick sheets with fully TIG-welded or vulcanized seams, eliminating the corrosion and solder failure common in standard gutters.

Redundant Emergency Scuppers

Every commercial roof design integrates secondary scuppers elevated 50mm above main drainage outlets, instantly discharging catastrophic deluge rain before weight collapses the roof deck.
